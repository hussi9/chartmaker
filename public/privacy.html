ChartGenie privacy

Charts, images and brand settings are processed and saved in your browser. An optional account does not upload or synchronize them. Export a backup before clearing browser storage.

Share links include chart data in the URL so our server can render a preview. Anyone with the link can read that chart. Do not share confidential data this way.

Optional accounts

AImasterz uses Supabase for shared account identity, including your email and authentication records. ChartGenie stores product-specific membership and plan records. A secure, HttpOnly session cookie keeps this portal signed in for at most 15 minutes; other product sessions are separate. Signing out here does not sign you out of other products.

Guest chart tools do not require registration. The account page does not run marketing analytics or chat widgets.

Optional measurement

On approved public pages, Google Analytics 4 loads with Consent Mode v2: analytics measurement is on by default, so an ordinary visit is counted, and Google may set analytics cookies. Advertising signals stay denied and are never enabled. Google receives a fixed allowlisted page name and path, with query strings and fragments removed and concrete chart ids mapped to their route pattern, plus bounded product events such as export requests. If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, the analytics tag does not load at all.

Use Privacy choices in the app to turn measurement off or back on; guest tools work either way. Vercel page measurement follows the same choice and gets the same route patterns. Turning measurement off stops every send from this browser, including Vercel measurement. Account, administration, shared-chart and unknown routes are excluded, as are URLs with queries or fragments. We do not send chart titles, labels, values, captions, file names, share-link data or free text.

Google and Vercel may process technical network information such as IP addresses under their policies. Withdrawal stops new sends without clearing your charts; it does not delete data already sent. Your preference is stored locally in this browser, separately for this product.

For account support, access or deletion requests, email admin@aimasterz.com. Do not send passwords or confidential charts. Shared-identity deletion may affect other AImasterz products; we will clarify the scope before acting.
