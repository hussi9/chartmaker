SignatureMaker · holistic reimagining

Sign once. Have it ready everywhere.

v3 fixed the shell. The remaining problem is the product shape: seven tools around a single image, and a real signature that people paste into public graphics without thinking. The reimagining makes the signature a kit (full, initials, compact, light and dark), splits a private version from a display version, and puts the everyday jobs (sign a PDF, sign off a document, an email signature, a stamped post) one click from home.

Is v3 good enough?

Its layout is. What holds the product back is scope and trust, not chrome:

  1. The kit turns one image into the set people actually need (initials, inline, dark) and makes edits propagate.
  2. Private vs display is the trust feature: it stops people posting their legal signature on Instagram without saying no to them.
  3. Jobs on Home replace “which tool?” with “what are you doing today?”, and remember the last file per job.
  4. Sign-off and email are the two most common real uses of a signature image and today need three other apps.
  5. Phone pad, refine, pen replay lift quality of the signature itself, the thing every output inherits.

What stays true: free, no account, local processing, no verification or audit-trail claims. Phone hand-off is the one feature that needs a relay; the .sigkit export is the no-server alternative.
